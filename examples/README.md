# Example builds

Three small projects built on 2026-10-22 by TDD agents that had the v1 entry snippet in their instructions and were
not told to write coded comments: `fib-cli` (Python), `ratelimiter-go` (Go), `ledger-ts` (TypeScript and SQLite).

They are kept unedited as evidence, not as reference style. Their coded lines use the legacy unspaced marker, and most
files also carry the prose comments and docstrings the protocol removes. What they showed, and what changed because of
it (spaced markers, `test:` anchors, the `why` form, prose signals, tests in scope, `=>` binding loosest), is in
`docs/open-questions.md`, `docs/grammar-examples.md`, `.analysis/ab/` and `.analysis/readability/`.
